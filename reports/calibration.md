## 4. Verdict Bands

| Score range | Verdict | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 0 – 20 | Safe | Benign archetypes score below this band across categories |
| 21 – 45 | Low | Mild context mismatches without exfiltration combos |
| 46 – 70 | Medium | Suspicious mismatches and/or single exfil combos |
| 71 – 89 | High | Multiple exfil combos or strong ML+static agreement |
| 90 – 100 | Critical | Threat-intel match or extreme multi-signal evidence |

Bands are held constant by golden regression tests (`tests/test_golden.py`); any recalibration must re-verify the demo scenarios in the same commit.
